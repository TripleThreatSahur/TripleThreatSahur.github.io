Old-Fashioned Pancakes

Stars: 4

Prep Time: 5 minutes

Cook Time: 15 minutes

Total Time: 20 minutes

Servings: 8

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups of all-purpose flour
  • 3.5 teaspoons of baking powder
  • 1 tablespoon of white sugar
  • 0.25 teaspoons or more of salt (preference)
  • 1.25 cups of milk
  • 3 tablespoons of melted butter
  • 1 large egg

Directions

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Mix flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt in a large bowl.
  3. Add milk, melted butter, and the egg. Mix until smooth.
  4. Heat a lightly oiled griddle or pan between medium and high heat. Pour or scoop the batter with a 1/4 measuring cup. Cook until bubbles form and the edges are dry. Usually 2 to 3 minutes.)
  5. Flip and cook until browned on the other side. Repeat with remaining batter.
  6. Enjoy your pancakes!
