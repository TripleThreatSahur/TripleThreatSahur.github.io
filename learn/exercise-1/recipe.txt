Title: Old-Fashioned Pancakes
Stars: 4
Prep Time: 5 minutes
Cook Time: 15 minutes
Total Time: 20 minutes
Servings: 8

Ingredients:
- 1.5 cups of all-purpose flour
- 3.5 teaspoons of baking powder
- 1 tablespoon of white sugar
- 0.25 teaspoons or more of salt (preference)
- 1.25 cups of milk
- 3 tablespoons of melted butter
- 1 large egg

Directions:
- Step 1: Gather all ingredients.
- Step 2: Mix flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt in a large bowl.
- Step 3: Add milk, melted butter, and the egg. Mix until smooth.
- Step 4: Heat a lightly oiled griddle or pan between medium and high heat. Pour or scoop the batter with a 1/4 measuring cup. Cook until bubbles form and the edges are dry. Usually 2 to 3 minutes.
- Step 5: Flip and cook until browned on the other side. Repeat with remaining batter.
- Step 6: Enjoy your pancakes!
